# The printed mount: first concept (4 Oct 2026)

Parts that put the Maker Arm, its Raspberry Pi and the bin on Barrymore's back. Spec:
[3JA] (the printed mount). Generated by code (`parts.py`, every dimension in `config.py`, read from `params/robot.yaml` where it is a fact
about the robot); regenerate with `python cad/mount/build.py` (DimOS venv, `pip install -e .[cad]`).

## Files in `print/`

Each part comes as **`.3mf`**, already placed on the bed the way it should print (open in Bambu Studio), and as
**`.step`** in the robot's frame (millimetres; X forward, Y left, Z up, origin at the body centre), for Fusion and for
the finite-element analysis. None of them contains any of Unitree's geometry.

| Part | What it does | On the bed | Size on the bed (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `adapter` | Stays on the robot. Bolts through the four rear holes of the back's top cover; carries the module and the bin | upside down (deck on the bed) | 245 x 228 x 29 |
| `module` | Comes off. The arm's base bolts on top; the Pi, buck converter, CAN adapter, speaker and mic sit in the bays underneath | upside down (arm seat on the bed) | 154 x 230 x 32 |
| `bench_plate` | The adapter's interface on a plate for the bench: the module bolts to it the same way | upside down | 150 x 240 x 22 |
| `bin` | Behind the module, held by magnets, pulls off | upright | 120 x 150 x 102 |
| `hat_seat` | The bowler's magnetic seat, screws to the module's top beside the arm | upright | 46 x 46 x 8 |
| `coupon` | Test holes. **Print this first** in the chosen filament and fit the bolts, inserts, dowels and magnets | upright | 160 x 48 x 10 |

Nothing needs splitting: every part fits the X1's 256 mm cube with at least 11 mm to spare.

## Print settings

- **Material:** a polycarbonate. The estimates in `report.json` cover Polymaker PolyMax PC, Bambu PC and 3DXTech
  CarbonX ezPC+CF (`cad/materials/`). CarbonX PC+CF is about 3.4 times stiffer along the layers by the long-term design
  values, for 14% more mass, and needs the X1C's hardened nozzle. The bin and the hat seat carry little load, so PETG
  will do for them.
- **Dry the filament** to the maker's recipe and print from a dry box. PC that has picked up water loses about 30% of
  its strength ([Xtj]).
- **Walls 8** (3.4 mm at a 0.42 mm line), **top and bottom 18 layers** at 0.2 mm (3.6 mm), **infill 15% gyroid**. The
  walls and skins carry the load; the infill only holds them apart. Fewer walls or skins makes the adapter too soft and
  too highly stressed when walking (see `report.json`).
- **Orientation:** as placed in the 3MF. The adapter and the module print upside down, so the arm's bending runs along
  the layers, not across them. Their undersides print as tops, so they need no support. The only bridges are the roofs of
  holes, pockets and the connector windows, each 16 mm across or less.
- **Supports:** none.
- **Brim:** recommended on the adapter and the bench plate (large flat PC parts lift at the corners).

## Hardware

- **Robot to adapter:** 4 bolts through the cover's 7.2 mm holes, assumed to be **M4**. The CAD doesn't model the
  thread, so measure it first and set `REAR_BOLT` in `config.py` to match.
- **Module to adapter (and to the bench plate):** 4 x M5 socket heads from the top, into **M5 heat-set inserts**
  (6.4 mm hole, 9.5 mm deep). The front two are on the ears beside the arm base. There are also 2 x 6 mm steel dowel pins
  pressed into the adapter, which locate in a round hole and a slot in the module. There is one power connector (an XT30
  panel mount) and one data connector, both in the module's side walls.
- **Arm base to module:** the base's own 4 x M5 on its 79 mm square, from below into the base's nut traps. These stay
  done up: the arm comes off with the module.
- **Bin:** 4 pairs of Ø10 x 3 mm N42 disc magnets (supermagnete S-10-03-N or equal). Glue them flush, 0.3 mm below the
  surface, with opposite poles facing. There are also 2 x 10 mm **stainless 304** (non-magnetic) balls glued half-proud
  into the adapter's deck. The bin sits on them with a cone socket and a V-slot, so it locates the same way every time and
  slides off them in a knock.
- **Hat seat:** 2 x M3 into heat-set inserts in the module's top, and 1 magnet in the boss. The hat needs a magnet or a
  steel disc in its crown.
- **Pi 3B:** M2.5 self-tapping screws into the four stand-offs.

## What the numbers say (estimates, before printing)

See `report.json` (masses, stiffness, stresses, magnets, clearances) and `load_cases.json` (the loads and boundary
conditions for the finite-element analysis, [tY3]). Every number there is an estimate until a printed part is weighed
and load-tested. Log each print and its results ([uip]).
