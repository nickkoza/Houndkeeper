# Research write-ups

These are readable accounts of problems worth learning from: a simulation clock that flattered every result, a
benchmark replay that was kinder than the live robot, a sensitivity study that found the sim had never delayed its
camera frames, and a detector whose misses were mostly its prompt. Each is dated and records what was true that day;
the specs hold what was decided from them, and each file opens with why it is kept. The 3 Oct 2026 audits (the Railyard gap, the spec-test and test-quality reviews, the arm host red team, the sim kindness audit) are the record of how this repository checks itself.
